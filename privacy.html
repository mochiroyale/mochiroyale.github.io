Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Mochi Royale is made by an independent developer. It's a game you play against computer opponents on your own device.

What we collect: nothing

Mochi Royale has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game doesn't send any data to us or to anyone else, and it works fully offline.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings (theme, sound volume, trash-talk level), your chosen flavour and your Ladder progress on your device so they're there next time. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

TestFlight beta

If you test a beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send (screenshots, comments) with us. That is covered by Apple's TestFlight terms and privacy policy. We use it only to fix and improve the game.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or trackers, and it loads nothing from other servers. Like any website, the company that hosts it may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service.

Changes

If this changes (for example, if we add crash reporting), we'll update this policy and the date above before that version ships.
