FAQ

How do I play Royale?
  1. Tap PLAY, then Royale: a Quick match, or the Ladder to climb level by level.
  2. Each turn, tap a tile up to 2 away to hop there.
  3. Power tiles go off as you land. Some attack (Snack Attack, Kine Mallet, Steam Burst), some help (Shell, Refill).
  4. Cracked tiles fall away. The last mochi standing wins.
How do I play Mochi Rush?
  1. Tap PLAY, then Mochi Rush.
  2. Hop up to 2 tiles at a time. The first mochi to the top wins.
  3. Items go off as you land: Boosts, Shells, Freezers and more.
  4. Stay ahead of the caramel Melt rising from below. Sooner or later, it RUSHes!
How do I change the theme, sound or other settings?

Tap ⚙ on the home screen (top left) or in the bar at the top of a match. There you can:

  • pick the Theme: Freezer or Sky;
  • turn Sound on or off, set the Volume and switch Haptics;
  • set the bots' Trash talk to Spicy, Mild or Off;
  • turn effects like screen shake, slow-mo and the 3-2-1 intro on or off;
  • replay How to play for either mode.

Changes apply at once and are saved on your phone.

How do I make the bots easier or harder?

After you tap PLAY, choose Easy, Normal or Hard under Bots at the top of the mode screen.

How do I change my mochi?

On the home screen, tap ‹ or › next to your mochi's name to pick Chocolate, Matcha, Mango or Ube. The bots take the other three.

How do I send TestFlight feedback?
  • From the game: take a screenshot while you play, then tap Share Beta Feedback and add a note.
  • From the TestFlight app: open TestFlight, tap Mochi Royale, then Send Beta Feedback.
  • By email: moltychat@gmail.com.

We most want to know: was it fun, did matches feel too short or too long, and which mode would you replay?

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. Mochi Royale works fully offline, with no accounts. Your settings and Ladder progress are saved on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.
